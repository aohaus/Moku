❓ How to Play
Moku doesn't just govern this town — Moku lives here. Each level gives you one goal: build up the town, but keep enough forest and parks standing to keep Nature healthy. Let Nature drop too far and both the economy and Moku's mood suffer. Leveling up unlocks new buildings like the School and Landmark — and watch out for the occasional fire, flood, storm, or earthquake, which can damage buildings and cost coins.
